## The Shift from Data Science to AI Engineering

While data science focuses on experimentation and model creation, AI engineering emphasizes:

- **Production readiness**: Models that work reliably in real-world conditions
- **Scalability**: Systems that handle increasing demand
- **Observability**: Understanding what your models do in production
- **Deployment automation**: CI/CD pipelines for ML models

## Tools You'll Encounter

**MLflow** - Experiment tracking and model registry  
**Docker/Kubernetes** - Containerization and orchestration  
**Kafka/Pulsar** - Stream processing for real-time inference  
**Prometheus/Grafana** - Metrics and monitoring  

> **Remember**: The best models mean nothing if they can't scale in production.
